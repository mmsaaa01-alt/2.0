Prime Factorization

    Definition 2.2 (Prime Factorization): The expression of a composite number as a product of prime numbers is called prime factorization.

    Process of Prime Factorization:
        Start by finding a prime factor of the composite number.
        Divide the number by that prime factor.
        If the quotient is also a composite number, repeat the process with the quotient.
        Continue until all factors are prime numbers.
        Express the original number as a product of all these prime factors.

    Theorem 2.2 (Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic): Every composite number can be expressed (factored) as a product of primes, and this factorization is unique, apart from the order in which the prime factors occur.
