Unit 3: Solving Equations

    Overview: Equations function like a balanced weighing scale, where the expression on one side of the equal sign must equal the value on the other side. This unit covers linear equations in one variable, systems of linear equations in two variables, non-linear equations (absolute value, exponents, and radicals), quadratic equations, and their practical real-life applications.

Steps for Solving Linear Equations in One Variable

To find the value of the variable that satisfies a linear equation, follow these sequential steps:

    Clear Fractions: Use the Least Common Multiple (LCM) of the denominators to eliminate fractions if present.
    Simplify Both Sides: Expand brackets, remove parentheses, and combine like terms on each side of the equation.
    Isolate the Variable: Use addition or subtraction properties to collect terms containing the variable on one side and constant terms on the other, then use multiplication or division to solve for the variable.
    Verify the Answer: Substitute the calculated value back into the original equation to ensure the Left-Hand Side (LHS) equals the Right-Hand Side (RHS).
